Step 2: Product In Action
HOW does your product work? Describe the process/method of what it does and the outcome it provides. Then provide a 6th-grade reading level version.
(Example: "The probiotics colonize the gut, which improves digestion, which reduces bloating")
Technical version: The SliceSafe Pro uses a "Hands-On-Top Safety Path" design where vegetables are placed in a container section and pushed through ultra-sharp stainless steel blades using a top-mounted handle. This keeps hands completely above and away from the blade path during the entire slicing motion. The sharp blade requires minimal pressure, which reduces slipping and "resistance moments" that cause most mandoline injuries. Food drops directly into a collection area below, creating uniform thickness slices in seconds.
6th-grade version: You put the vegetable in the top, push down with the handle, and perfectly thin slices come out the bottom—your hands never go near the blade. It's like a safe, fast way to make restaurant-looking salads and slaws without any knife skills.
Step 3: Features
What are your product's main features? (Add as few or as many as you need)
1. Hands-On-Top Safety Path Design — Your hands stay on the top handle, completely away from the blade path during slicing.
2. Ultra-Sharp Stainless Steel Blade — Surgical-grade sharpness requires less pressure, reducing slips and "resistance moments."
3. Non-Slip Stable Base — Rubberized feet prevent movement during slicing, even with hard vegetables like potatoes and sweet potatoes.
4. Adjustable Thickness Dial — Simple dial settings from paper-thin to thicker slices for different recipes (salads, chips, gratins).
5. Direct-to-Container Slicing — Slices drop directly into a collection area or your own bowl, reducing mess and speeding up workflow.
6. Rinse-Clean Workflow — Minimal parts and open design allows quick rinse + brush cleaning without putting fingers near blades.
7. Compact/Foldable Storage — Stores flat or upright, doesn't become a "drawer gadget" that never gets used.

Step 6: New Mechanisms (USPs)
USP: Unique selling proposition. Aka, what makes your product unique? Does your product have any NEW mechanisms that separate it from the market? (Add as many as you need)
1. "Hands-On-Top Safety Path" — Unlike traditional mandolines where hands move toward the blade, our design keeps hands on a top-mounted handle while food travels through the blade path below. This is a fundamentally different motion pattern that eliminates the "guard + freehand" failure mode.
2. "Sharp-Is-Safer" Blade System — Most injuries happen when dull blades require extra force, causing slips during resistance. Our surgical-grade blade cuts with minimal pressure, so there's no "push hard → slip → cut" pattern.
3. "Safe-Clean Path" Design — Open rinse channels + included brush means cleaning never requires fingers near blades. This directly addresses the #2 purchase objection in the category.

Answer these to help AI identify mechanisms you might be blind to:
1. What does your product do that you DON'T advertise?

The bundle includes a verified cut-resistant glove (not the "useless trash" gloves many competitors include), a cleaning brush specifically designed for the blade area, and a Quick-Start Safety Card with clear "do this / don't do that" instructions. Executive Summary
This document provides a single Desire-Based Core Avatar with multiple Sub-Avatars for SliceSafe Pro, following the Avatar Module framework for Stage 1 brands ($0-100k/month).
Key Principle: Your Core Avatar should be built around ONE element - ideally a DESIRE. Sub-Avatars then narrow this down by adding Experiences, Emotions, Behaviors, and Demographics. The lower your revenue, the MORE specific your Sub-Avatars need to be.
Market Sophistication Level: Stage 3-4 (highly sophisticated). Consumers have heard safety claims before and are deeply skeptical. Standard claims like "safe guard" no longer create belief. Success requires explaining WHY this mandoline is different through new mechanisms.

CORE AVATAR
Desire-Based Core Avatar
Core Desire: "I want fast, easy, uniform vegetable prep for home cooking."
This is the raw, unfiltered desire that unites ALL potential buyers. It contains no demographics, no specific experiences, no emotions - just the core "I want" statement that drives purchase intent in this category.
Why This Desire Was Chosen
Testing against the 3 Dimensions of Desire Power:
Urgency: HIGH - Felt every time someone needs to cook (daily for most people)
Staying Power: HIGH - This desire renews continuously; people will always need to prep food
Scope: HIGH - Millions of home cooks share this desire across all demographics
Core Instincts This Desire Connects To
This surface-level desire taps into deeper, permanent human instincts:
Health: "Fast prep = I'll actually eat vegetables = I stay healthy"
Comfort: "Easy prep = cooking doesn't feel like work = less stress"
Control: "Uniform slices = predictable results = I feel competent"
Status: "Restaurant-quality presentation = I look like a good cook"
The Category's Unique Barrier
Unlike most kitchen tools, mandolines have a massive emotional blocker sitting between the desire and the purchase: Fear of injury. This is why safety-focused Sub-Avatars are so critical for this product - they directly address the #1 reason people DON'T buy despite having the core desire.

HOW SUB-AVATARS WORK
Sub-Avatars take the Core Desire and narrow it down by adding elements from the other 4 Avatar Categories:
The Formula: Core Desire + Experience + Emotion + Behavior (+ Demographics if needed) = Highly Specific Sub-Avatar
Stage 1 Rule: The more categories you combine, the more specific (and relatable) your Sub-Avatar becomes. At Stage 1, you NEED this specificity because you can't out-spend competitors - you have to out-relate them.

SUB-AVATARS
Each Sub-Avatar starts with the Core Desire ("I want fast, easy, uniform vegetable prep") and adds specificity through the other categories.
Testing Strategy: Test ONE Sub-Avatar at a time for 2-4 weeks. Go deep before going wide. When something works, add more layers to that winning Sub-Avatar rather than jumping to a new one.
