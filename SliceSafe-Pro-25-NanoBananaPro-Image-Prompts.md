## Generation Tips

1. **Always attach reference images** of the actual SliceSafe Pro product for color/shape accuracy

3. **Don't over-prompt** -- Nano Banana Pro understands natural language; be descriptive, not repetitive
4. **Edit, don't re-roll** -- if an image is 80% right, ask for the specific change
5. **Add text overlays in Figma/Canva** -- don't bake text into the AI generation prompt
