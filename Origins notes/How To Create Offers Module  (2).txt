Why Offers Have Such A Big Impact:
Offers are the most important part of the Landing Page 


Why You MUST Know Your Numbers With New Offers:
Important Note #1:
Offers can be like drugs… They are ADDICTING. They can take your ROAS from a 2x to 4x overnight, pump your conversion rate from 1.5% to 2.5%, and drive revenue through the roof… But they can oftentimes be IMPOSSIBLE to get off of and can be OVERLY relied on to acquire customers 
Important Note #2
Prior to setting up any offer, ALWAYS use the profit calculator to establish the new targets for ROAS & CP,A so you know whether or not it is successful 

The Types Of Offers You Can Run:
Bundle and Save (Favorite):
Group commonly purchased products and bundle together at a discount.
Provide a REASON for the bundle and think of a name for the bundle as well 
Can raise AOV if you contact supplier and ship the “add ons” in one package
Example:
2-Pack or 3-Pack 
“The Staple 6-Pack | Regular $265 Now For $186.00
“Rat Protection Bundle” | Rat Vac + Capz (Free) + Vent(Free) + eBook(Free) | Regular $371 now $249
$X Amount off Or Save X%
“Save $20!” or “Save 10%”
“Save $30 on First Orders!" or “Save 25% On First Orders!”
“Save Up To $80! SiteWide” Or “Save up to 80% SiteWide” (Discount Select products or variants)
Use law of large numbers
If % is higher the $ off use % vice versa.



Buy X Get X Free
“Buy 1 Get 1 Free”
“Buy 2 Get 1 Free”
Buy X Get Another Product Free”

Buy X Get Y% Off:
“Buy 1 Get 50% Off!”
Buy 1 Get X Products 15% Off”

Free Shipping:
“Free Shipping On All Orders”
“Free Shipping On First Orders”
“Free Shipping when you spend X”
“Free Expedited Shipping”
