## THE PRODUCT

**SliceSafe Pro Mandoline** — A vertical standing mandoline slicer.
- Light-blue, 12" tall vertical tower design
- Ergonomic pressing handle on TOP
- Transparent food hopper in the middle
- V-blade ENCLOSED inside the housing
- Rubber non-slip base
- Clear catch tray with lid ($12.99 value) + cleaning brush ($11.99 value) included
- **Price: $64.99 (was $99.95) — SAVE 35%**

### The Mechanism: "Hands-On-Top" Design
Your hands grip the top handle and push straight down. The blade is enclosed in the mid-section. Your hands are physically 8+ inches above the blade. Hands CANNOT reach the blade during normal use. Safety is built into the DESIGN, not dependent on a guard, gloves, or discipline.

### Why This Mechanism Matters (Origins Framework)
This is a GENUINE new mechanism in a Stage 3-4 sophisticated market. Traditional mandolines = flat blade, hands move toward it, guard exists but nobody uses it. SliceSafe = vertical design, hands stay on top, blade enclosed below. This isn't a "better guard." It's a fundamentally different approach. This provides NEW HOPE — the required strategic response at Stage 3-4.

- Health/Safety is the STRONGEST permanent force (rank 1 of 6)
- Physical product = what you ship. Functional product = what it DOES (what they're actually buying)

### Testing Method: Marksman (for statics)
- 3 creatives × 2 body copies × 2 headlines
- Test best angle for each sub-avatar
- Kill: CTR < 1.5% after 3 days + $15 minimum spend
- Scale: Purchase → increase budget 20% after 3 days consistency

### Ad Copy Rules (from data):
1. **Assertion hooks ONLY** — never open with a question
2. **Numbers stop the scroll** — use specific numbers (8 inches, 90 seconds, $64.99)
3. **3-beat rhythm** — [Number]. [Quality]. [Safety punchline].
4. **Warm lifestyle > studio shots**
5. **Max 3 feature points per creative**
6. **Relief/discovery energy > educational energy**
7. **SHOW vs TELL** — describe BEHAVIORS not features ("Holding my breath" > "safety guard")
8. **FRAMES system** — every creative needs at least 2 of: SAFE, EASY, NEW, FAST
9. **Self-categorization avoidance** — never call it "a mandoline" in the hook (triggers the fear mental model before they see our design)
10. **Awareness-matched copy length** — short for Product Aware, long for Problem Aware

### Hook Specifications (from swipe file visual audit):
- **Size:** 48px+ Bold (minimum). Swipe file winners average 60-80px Bold.
- **Length:** 2-7 words for the main scroll-stop line
- **Weight:** Always Bold or Extra-Bold. Never Regular or Semi-bold for headlines.
